    What it checks

    1. SupportEvery sentence in the answer is covered by a passage. Invented sentences fail.
    2. ScopeThe answer doesn't widen who a rule is for. "All employees" fails a rule that applies only to full-time staff.
    3. QualifierLimits, numbers and amounts survive. "Nach der Probezeit", "bis zu 1.200 €", "after the probation period".
    4. OmissionExceptions survive. "Unless", "es sei denn", "außer", "sofern" and the clause after them.

    How it judges and what it misses. It reads words, not meaning, so a correct paraphrase can fail. The limits page shows a real case.
